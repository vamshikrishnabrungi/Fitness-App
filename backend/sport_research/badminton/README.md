# Badminton Research Pack

This folder contains the app-facing badminton knowledge export generated by
`backend/ingest_badminton_teaching_database.py`.

## Scope

The first badminton pack covers:

- rules, rally scoring, match IQ, and serve/receive context
- grip changes and racket control
- serve and return
- clear, drop, smash, drive, lift, block, and net-shot families
- split-step, lunge, footwork, recovery, and base-position logic
- singles, doubles, and mixed doubles tactical differences
- badminton-specific strength and conditioning
- injury and load management for ankle, knee, Achilles, shin, shoulder, wrist/elbow, and low back
- beginner, intermediate, and advanced teaching progressions
- level transition and hold/regress gates

## Collections Produced

- `sport_profiles`
- `sport_roles`

- `planning_rules`

- `knowledge_sources`

## Notes

This pack teaches the AI what badminton quality to train, why, and when. Strength
exercise execution details still come from the general exercise, mobility, and
plyometric libraries.

Future expansion should add deeper records for individual stroke mechanics,
deception, shot combinations, doubles rotation systems, and tournament planning.
