# Boxing Sport Research

This folder stores source-backed boxing research drafts before they are converted into app-facing SFTC database records.

Backend sport key: `boxing`.

## Pipeline

1. Research boxing as a complete sport.
2. Create structured draft JSON files in `drafts/`.
3. Validate drafts with `validate_boxing_research_drafts.py`.
4. Summarize drafts with `summarize_boxing_research_drafts.py`.
5. Convert approved drafts into app-facing Mongo records.

## Wave 1 Domains

- Rules, ring model, scoring, and competition structure
- Technical fundamentals: stance, guard, footwork, punches, defense
- Tactical IQ: range, rhythm, pressure, counterpunching, southpaw/orthodox, corner strategy
- Boxing S&C, injury risk, sparring load, weight making, and macro planning
- Level-based teaching progressions and assessments
