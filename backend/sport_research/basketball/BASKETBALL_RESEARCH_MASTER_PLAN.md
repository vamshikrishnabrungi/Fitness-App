# Basketball Research Master Plan

## Objective

Build an app-facing basketball knowledge base with the same quality bar as the cricket, volleyball, and football pipelines.

The database should help SFTC understand basketball as a complete sport: rules, court zones, roles, offensive and defensive skill, team tactics, S&C, injury risk, macro planning, and level-based teaching.

## Research Domains

1. Rules, positions, court zones, and game model
2. Offensive skills: ball handling, passing, shooting, finishing, footwork, spacing, pick-and-roll
3. Defensive skills, rebounding, screen navigation, transition, and team tactics
4. Basketball S&C, injury risk, load management, return-to-play, and macro planning
5. Level-based teaching progressions and assessments

## App-Facing Collections To Create

- `sport_profiles`
- `sport_roles`
- `sport_training_rules`
- `planning_rules`
- `sport_teaching_progressions`
- `sport_skill_assessments`
- `sport_level_transition_rules`

## Quality Rules

- Use credible sources only.
- Preserve source refs in every final record.
- Prefer structured coaching rules over long prose.
- Keep raw research drafts separate from app-facing retrieval records.
- Teach beginner, intermediate, and advanced players differently.
- Connect technical skill to tactical decision-making and physical demands.
- Connect S&C to court demands, not generic gym work.
