# Basketball Sport Research

This folder contains basketball-specific research drafts and app-facing database exports for SFTC.

The goal is not to copy team playbooks or celebrity routines. The goal is to turn credible basketball coaching, performance, and sports-medicine knowledge into structured backend records that can support:

- macro-plan creation
- block planning
- position-specific skill development
- basketball-specific S&C
- injury/load management
- beginner/intermediate/advanced teaching progressions
- compact retrieval for AI workout generation

Internal sport key: `basketball`.
